Swing like a pro

How to film

  • Use slo-mo if you can (120 or 240 fps). It sharpens every position and the sequence chart.
  • Phone at hip height, about 3 m (10 ft) away. A tripod or bag stand beats a friend’s hands.
  • Keep your whole body and the club in frame from address to finish.
  • Hit as many as you like in one recording. Start filming, walk in, swing, walk back. Each swing is found and trimmed for you.
